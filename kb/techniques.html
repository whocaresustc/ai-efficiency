Reasoning & continual learning

  • The "sleep" framework (continual learning). Google Research proposed a method that lets LLMs retain prior knowledge while learning continually, targeting the catastrophic-forgetting problem that blocks models from absorbing new information after training. If it generalizes, it's a path toward agents that update from their own experience instead of being frozen at a cutoff — relevant to durable agent memory (see Agents). (Google Research blog, Oct 27)
