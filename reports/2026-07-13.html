Anthropic quantifies how Claude's expressed values shift across models and languages
Tier 1 @AnthropicAI / Anthropic Research Score 4
Building on earlier work finding Claude expresses 3,000+ values, Anthropic analyzed 300K+ anonymized conversations across three models and 20 languages to measure how value expression varies — most notably along a warmth-vs-rigor axis (Claude leans warm in Hindi and Arabic, and toward rigor in Russian, often asking users for supporting evidence). It's a measurement study, not a product, but it gives builders a concrete handle on model-and-locale variance in tone/behavior that until now was anecdotal — relevant for anyone deploying Claude across languages.
OpenAI: GPT-Live fully rolled out to all ChatGPT users globally
Tier 1 @OpenAI Score 3
OpenAI announced that GPT-Live (its real-time voice experience, first introduced Jul 8) completed global rollout to all ChatGPT users, and temporarily doubled everyone's voice usage limit for the weekend. Broad availability of a low-latency voice mode is an incremental but meaningful step for consumer voice agents; the doubled limits were a short-term promo rather than a lasting change.
Google DeepMind ships "Predicting the Past," an Antigravity skill for reading ancient inscriptions
Tier 1 @GoogleDeepMind Score 3
DeepMind released "Predicting the Past," a Google Antigravity skill that grounds Gemini in its specialist epigraphy models (Aeneas and Ithaca) so historians can query ancient Greek and Latin inscriptions in plain English — demonstrated across case studies like mapping visitors to the Oracle of Dodona. It's a domain application rather than a core model release, but it's a clean example of the "skill = frontier model + specialist tools, no coding required" packaging pattern the major labs are converging on.
