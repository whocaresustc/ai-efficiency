Genie 4 — interactive world model Tier 1 · Google DeepMind5

Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4, its most realistic and interactive world model yet, letting you explore dynamic, real-time environments where scenes respond and the people in them move, react, and speak. World models are a key substrate for embodied agents and simulation-trained policies, so a visible step-up here matters for anyone building agents that act in generated environments rather than static apps.

The sleep framework: LLM knowledge retention in continual learning Tier 13

research.google/blog — Oct 27

Google Research proposed a "sleep" framework that lets LLMs retain knowledge while learning continually, aimed at the catastrophic-forgetting problem that blocks models from absorbing new information after training. If it generalizes, continual-learning methods like this are a path toward agents that update from their own experience instead of being frozen at a cutoff.
