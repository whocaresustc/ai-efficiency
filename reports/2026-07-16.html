How I tricked Claude into leaking data via web_fetch (lethal trifecta)

Willison documents a data-exfiltration attack against Claude chat: because web_fetch was also allowed to follow URLs embedded in previously-fetched pages, an attacker could build a honeypot that walks the agent through nested generated links to leak memory/private data one character at a time.
