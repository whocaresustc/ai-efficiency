Tier 2 · Research Google Research Score 4 Oct 28 (blog Oct 27)

The "Sleep" framework: continual learning without catastrophic forgetting

Google Research (Behrouz, Mirrokni et al.) introduced a biologically inspired "sleep" phase that lets a deployed LLM periodically consolidate short-term context into long-term parameters and then train on self-generated "dream" data, keeping near-zero forgetting across sequential tasks (e.g. learning unseen languages like Manchu and Kalamang back-to-back) where ICL/SFT baselines collapse. It matters because it attacks the static-knowledge-cutoff problem head-on — an agent that can integrate new facts post-deployment without retraining-from-scratch is a direct lever on long-running autonomous systems; announced on the @GoogleResearch timeline Oct 28, the exact first-party path added after the Harness² source-gap miss.
